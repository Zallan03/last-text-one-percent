## The entry

The last text of a dying phone, in the phone's own voice. It lists what it
kept safe — then dies mid-word, before it can finish the sentence.

> 1% left: I kept your 400 photos, your 37 alarms and your 2 a.m. searches safe, and now I die mid-sentence, unchar

## Run

`main.ts` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (113 characters, gate 1–200).

```sh
node main.ts
```
